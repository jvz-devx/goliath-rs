## Owned border delivery

E14's real client independently found that E10 never sent owned column (0,2).
No chunk at x=0 or z=0 arrived, and physics stopped in that unloaded column.
`Hub::next_batch` required light neighbours outside the tile. Those neighbours
correctly never load. The fix accepts the existing closed foreign boundary for
packet readiness only. It does not permit foreign simulation or writes.

The permanent borders fixture derives expected owned columns from geometry
and view distance 3, then reads chunk packets at every wall of every tile.
Movement and teleport acknowledgements are withheld during each probe.
`owned-borders.json` passed 16 walls, 384 expected column observations and zero
missing columns. Its 13.885-second run started at load 32.618 and ended at
33.419. `owned-borders-live-build.json` repeated the matrix.

The eager-reader terrain pilot completed 14 crossings with all 64 junction
columns. Its 641 packet/movement boundaries had zero missing visible columns
and zero visible reloads. A foreign block changed live; a foreign break was
refused. An entity stayed hidden until entering its owner, providing a positive
control. This is packet evidence, not final shared-reader or GPU-frame acceptance.

## Local lease authority

E13-1 and E13-2 were reproduced defects on E10. The writer checked only local
FENCE, and returning saved generations bypassed registration. E16 supplies the
store/model contract in `E16-leases.md`.

Horizon claims its own original-send lease. The launcher skips legacy Claim
for a leased binary and reads the granted fence through `cluster-authority`.
Renewal takes a ticket under a short keeper lock, performs store IO outside it,
then observes the real reply under a short lock. Local action checks never wait
behind renewal network IO. Expiry latches closed, including after healing.

The default is L=1,000 ms, margin=100 ms and renewal every 250 ms. Gates cover
login stages, ordinary and held placement, packet and game-event dispatch,
actual append, barrier, installation and acknowledgement, recovery writes and
save helpers. Expired processes keep diagnostics and explicit shutdown but
stop world ticks. A saved player generation cannot grant authority.

Clock checks compare bracketed Instant samples with a suspend-aware monotonic
clock. The local macOS clock_gettime manual distinguishes sleep-advancing
CLOCK_MONOTONIC from sleep-stopping uptime. Linux uses BOOTTIME. Wall time never
grants authority. Four integration tests cover expiry latching, discontinuity,
simulated suspension and critical budget. Actual OS suspension and Linux
runtime acceptance were not performed.

Critical IO reserves 100 ms inside the local deadline and checks after barriers.
This does not make APFS or scheduling bounded. An unbounded pause inside a
syscall or between a final check and its physical effect requires storage-side
fencing. No power-loss, Byzantine-process or unbounded-stall guarantee is claimed.

## Capacity interfaces and overload

The handover Meridian opens four eager readers per player at all distances,
including its own tile. E17 measured about eight threads per player, 0.0053
proxy cores per player at 50 and about 1.5 proxy cores at 190. E12 did not isolate
backend CPU per reader. E18 owns the shared/lazy implementation and its cost
proof. FINAL.json explicitly says the handover is not lazy.

Server status exposes exact per-guest placed, held and terrain_reader fields.
Additional metrics are frozen, released, tracked/queued chunks, view distance
and wire-byte counters. A frozen source can remain placed briefly during
handover; placed alone does not identify a second acting player.

An unimported, unplaced reader can request its own radius:

```text
player-observe UUID {"position":[128,-60,128],"radius":8}
```

The array form remains valid. Explicit radii 2 through 32 control the advertised
radius, tracker and actual loading area independently of real-player caps.
Array refresh retains the explicit radius. The reader remains unplaceable.

`observer-radius-8-v2.json` passed: four outer axial columns arrived, the reader
stayed held/unplaced, the real player stayed radius 3, and radius 33 and import
were refused. The immutable development engine is `radius-fe38586a5b21/`,
SHA-256 `fe38586a5b2103e1ee4e3ce3c2f7b63ec670abe950bda3b8bd5f7978fcb6f208`.

The launcher replaces the fixture's max_expected=8 with four times configured
max players, 512 at the default 128. Both values are configurable and recorded
in FINAL.json. Real admission bounds remain. E18 owns retry/defer classification;
the frozen proxy still latches some preparation refusals or closes on reader
setup failure.

The queue remains bounded at eight. The deterministic test blocks the worker,
fills it, then requests a departing-player cut. It returns cut queue is full
before export/freeze, frozen count stays zero and three later source movements
are accepted. Retry and abort release succeed after draining. Full-queue
refusal does not poison the writer.

Eight concurrent callers measured this full-barrier raw cut service on flat
terrain in the fast profile. Phase-file instrumentation from the saturation
control remained enabled. These are diagnostic, not untraced capacity rows:

## Failures kept

Terrain pilots withheld edges before the readiness fix. An old-client fixture
command caused a broken pipe and exposed unsafe inherited process-group cleanup.
E12 now stops its own child PID and preserves results even if cleanup fails.

Vehicle pilots caught unsupported remote-console mounting, empty-feedback
assumptions and an integer-coordinate filter. Summon coordinates were centred
at 1.5, not 1.0. Corrected real interaction packets and filters passed later.

The first live-state export ran before a new saved body existed. Registration
now initializes it through the normal player-turn path. The mixed-state fixture
initially compared stale fake-client flags; it now reads the authoritative
source snapshot, without changing the game. The first radius test changed
tracking but not loading. The first queue fixture omitted its gate environment
and was invalid. A small admission fixture began while an observer still held
a startup reservation; its failed attempt remains. The frozen eager proxy also
closed the client when reader setup hit admission overload. The backend-only
repeat disabled terrain setup to isolate Horizon. It passed refusal, continued
source input with no freeze and retry after the reservation was released.
Controller deferral remains E18's responsibility, not a handover-proxy claim.

Clippy caught an unnecessary unwrap, collapsible if and needless borrow. A
recovery build caught an ambiguous Result type. No suppression was added.
A few isolated startups returned errno 22 before level start, including one
radius partition attempt. Its cause is unresolved. Freshly named replacement
runs passed; failures remain and are never pooled into a successful row.
